The client ID and client secret are saved with Firefox Sync and can be restored on desktop devices signed into the same Mozilla account with Add-ons sync enabled. Each device still needs its own Google sign-in.

Credentials and tokens are unencrypted in each Firefox profile. OAuth access and refresh tokens stay on this device and are never put in Firefox Sync. Use Sign Out to remove tokens, and revoke access in your Google Account to invalidate them. See PRIVACY.md for details.
